# What is Train Sim World 6?

Train Sim World 6 is a realistic railway simulator with detailed locomotives, authentic routes and immersive driver controls.

It blends real-world signalling, weather and physics to make each journey feel like you are actually behind the controls.

---

# How the Interface Helps

This telemetry interface connects directly to the game and gives you live speed, route position, cruise control, weather tools and more.

Use it to monitor train performance and stay within the speed limits while you learn the routes.

---

# Start with the Training Centre

The Training Centre is the best place to begin in TSW6.

It teaches the basics of:
- Braking
- Throttle control
- Route knowledge

before you move on to full service operations.

If you are new to the simulator, head to the Training Centre first and then come back here to use the dashboard with confidence.
