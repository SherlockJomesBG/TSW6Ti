Mobile Device Notice

TSW6Ti is unable to connect to Train Sim World from this device.

Why can't I connect on mobile?

Connecting to your game requires direct local communication with Train Sim World 6 running on your PC (port 31270).

Modern mobile browsers on iOS, iPadOS, and Android do not support the local browser extension (TSW6Ti Local Connector) needed to securely bridge live telemetry from your game without routing through external servers.

Please open TSW6Ti on your PC or Mac using a desktop Chromium browser (such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Opera) where the connector extension can be installed.

Install TSW6Ti Local Connector

Connecting over HTTPS requires a Chromium-based desktop browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, or Vivaldi) with the local connector installed.

The lightweight TSW6Ti Local Connector bridges your browser directly to your game locally (port 31270) so live driving telemetry never passes through external servers.

Download for Chrome / Chromium
How to install in Chrome / Chromium:
  1. Download and extract the zip file above.
  2. In your browser, navigate to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).
  3. Toggle Developer mode ON (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
